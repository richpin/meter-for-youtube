# Privacy Policy — Meter for YouTube

_Last updated: 2026-07-25_

Meter for YouTube ("the extension") is built to respect your privacy. This
policy explains what data the extension handles and how.

## Summary

The extension does **not** collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal
data with the developer or any third party. Everything it records stays on
your own device and in your own Google account.

## What the extension records

To show you how much time you spend on YouTube, the extension records two
values while a YouTube tab is open:

- **Usage time** — how long a YouTube tab is visible and focused.
- **Watch time** — how long a video is actually playing.

These are stored only as daily totals (a number of seconds per date). The
extension also stores one setting: your chosen theme (auto, light, or dark).

## What the extension does NOT record

- No names, email addresses, or any personally identifiable information.
- No passwords, payment, health, or location data.
- No browsing history — it does **not** record which pages, videos, or URLs
  you visit, page titles, or search queries.
- No page content, clicks, keystrokes, or mouse activity.

## Where your data is stored

- **On your device** (`chrome.storage.local`) — the working copy of your
  daily totals.
- **In your own Google account** (`chrome.storage.sync`) — a backup so your
  history survives reinstalling the extension. This data lives in your Google
  account and is governed by Google's own privacy terms. The developer cannot
  access it.

No data is ever sent to the developer or to any external server. The
extension makes no network requests.

## Data retention

Daily totals older than 90 days are deleted automatically. You can remove all
stored data at any time by uninstalling the extension (local data is erased)
and clearing the extension's synced data from your Google account.

## Permissions

- **storage** — to save your daily totals and theme setting as described above.
- **Host access to `youtube.com`** — so the extension can display its overlay
  and measure time on YouTube pages. It is limited to YouTube and used for no
  other purpose.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same
